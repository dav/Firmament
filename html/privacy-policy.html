Privacy Policy for Dav's Firmament

Effective date: July 12, 2026

Firmament is an iPhone augmented-reality app that draws an imaginary solar-system lattice of markers over the live camera view of the sky, computed from your location and the current time.

The short version: Firmament collects nothing. The app makes no network requests, has no accounts, and sends no information anywhere. Everything it computes stays on your device.

1. Information Firmament Does Not Collect

We do not run any analytics, user-tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting services. We do not operate servers that store information about you. Firmament does not require an account or log-in, and the App Store version of the app makes no network connections of any kind.

2. Camera

Firmament uses your camera solely to display the live view of the sky that the lattice markers are drawn over, via Apple's ARKit framework. Camera frames are processed on your device in real time for display and device-motion tracking only. They are never recorded, stored, analyzed for any other purpose, or transmitted off the device.

3. Location

Your location, together with the current time, determines your position relative to the Sun — and therefore which lattice markers are above your horizon and where they appear in your sky. This computation happens entirely on your device. Your location is never stored beyond the current session and never leaves the device.

4. Information Stored On Your Device

The following preferences are kept locally on your iPhone and are never sent to Firmament's developer:

  • Display settings (layout mode, cube face colors, horizon options).
  • Whether you have already seen the intro tour.

5. Third-Party Services

None. Firmament contacts no third-party services. The solar ephemeris used to place the lattice is computed mathematically on your device, not fetched from a server.

6. Children's Privacy

Firmament collects no information from anyone, including children. The app contains no user-generated content, no social features, and no advertising.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
